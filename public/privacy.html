Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Monitor is a private, self-hosted application. Each installation is operated by one person, its owner, for that person's own use. This policy describes what an installation does with the data of the Google account its owner connects to it.

What data is accessed

Monitor requests one Google OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, and uses it for one call: reading the account's storage quota — its total limit and the space used by Drive, Gmail and Photos together.

Monitor does not read, list, download, create, modify or delete any file, email, photo or other content of the account, and it does not access the account holder's name, email address or contacts.

How the data is used

The quota figures are used only to compute the free space left in the account, to show it to the owner, and to alert the owner when it runs low.

Where the data is stored

The figures are stored in the owner's own Monitor database, on a server the owner controls. The OAuth credentials — the client's id and secret and the refresh token — are kept in a configuration file readable only by the Monitor service on that server. Nothing is sent to the developers of Monitor or to anyone else.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising, and not used to train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model. Monitor contains no analytics or tracking.

Limited Use

Monitor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The quota figures are kept as history for as long as the owner keeps the installation, and are deleted with its database. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that, Monitor can no longer read the quota.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be raised as an issue at github.com/pravbeseda/monitor/issues.
